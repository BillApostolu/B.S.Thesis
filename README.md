# Bachelor of Science Thesis

Welcome to the repository for my B.S. Thesis. This repository contains the final thesis document and abstract in English and Greek alongside the scripts utilised for this thesis and some examples of results.

This study develops a machine learning methodology to assess urban thermal risk in eight major Greek cities to assist with climate change adaptation.

-Tools Used: Google Earth Engine, QGIS, and K-means clustering.

-Key Indicators: Land surface temperature (LST), vegetation (NDVI), population, and warmest month mean maximum temperature.

-Results: Thermal risk is categorized into 5 to 7 classes and correlated with local climate zones to extract specific, actionable environmental parameters.
